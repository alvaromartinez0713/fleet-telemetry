Fleet telemetry · product family · vertical sheet

Three products, one data pipeline

The same telemetry travels through all three: the driver app captures and sends it, FleetGo Ops turns it into decisions about vehicles, and Fleet Platform explains what the system itself is doing. Nothing is duplicated between them — each screen answers a question the other two deliberately do not.

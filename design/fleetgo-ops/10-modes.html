  • keep

  • keep

What the agent will never absorb

  • rule
    The map. Spatial reading is not a conversation.
  • rule
    The alert queue. Three open alerts are glanced at, not asked about.
  • rule
    The scrubber. Exploring time is physical manipulation.
  • rule
    The approval gate. The agent proposes; a human clicks. Always.
  • rule
    The audit record. Dispatch and silence are written with who, when and why — a chat log is not an audit log.
The test to apply to every future screen: if the question it answers can be asked in a sentence, it belongs to the agent. If it needs to be seen (space, time, a queue) or proven (bytes, audit), it stays a surface.
